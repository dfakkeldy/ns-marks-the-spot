# Sheet 087 — Mount Thom, Westville, Stellarton and inland Pictou

**RMS 117.05 m exceeds 100 m; regional limitations remain.** 4 physical controls define the frozen similarity; 4 excluded checks give median 105.79 m, empirical P95 158.76 m, maximum 163.94 m. Bias: +19.72 m east, -29.99 m north. Distances are WGS84 geodesic ground metres. The active fit was frozen before its reported check scoring.

Original 6052 × 4138, 160 ppi JPEG extracted from https://novascotia.ca/natr/land/indexmaps/087.pdf. All source coordinates remain in the original frame. The separately accessible complete scan retains marginal attribution and warning.

Complete original mapped frame retains Mount Thom and Lower Mount Thom, Salt Springs, Limerock, Greenhill, Alma, Westville, Stellarton and New Glasgow, Millbrook, New Gairloch, Rocklin, Concord Mills, Hopewell, Eureka, Lansdowne, Glengarry, Lorne, Springville and every inland parcel/river region. No displaced inset identified.

A constrained reflected similarity was selected before any fitting or check scoring: four sparse controls cannot reliably support independent axis scales and shear. It estimates uniform scale, rotation and translation. The first fit and all four first check results remain unchanged.

Forbes Lake is coded WALK20 but its long southern lake-to-stream transition lacked a stable common endpoint; candidate C05 was rejected before fitting. Gair Loch lacked a clear matching closed modern outline, and ambiguous river/parcel crossings near Salt Springs and Hopewell were not forced into controls. All proposals and the pre-fit model rationale are retained.

Round Lake and Mill Pond southern targets were refined on their actual tips before fitting. Modern NSTDB pagination retrieved 6,497 features over four complete pages. Frozen sheet 088 supplied discovery guidance only. Native frame corners and all mapped content were inspected before masking.

Limitations:

- Four excluded checks give RMS 117.05 m, exceeding the 100 m ceiling. Median 105.79 m exceeds 75 m and P95 158.76 m exceeds 150 m; maximum is 163.94 m. The failed first result remains authoritative.
- All four checks share their lake with a control and are strongly spatially correlated. Only Botany Bay, Round, Calder and the Mill Pond near Concord Mills contribute observations. Whole-sheet accuracy is not established.
- The northern band, northeastern urban/industrial corridor and broad central region have no reliable independent validation. The constrained model does not resolve those evidence gaps.
- Modern WALK20 boundaries were used for retained observations. Mill Pond has 18 px working source uncertainty; the other lake points use 12 px. Full image retention does not imply geographic acceptance.
