Limitations:

- Checks sample western lake cohorts and coastal headlands; urban/industrial, parcel-interior and most eastern offshore regions remain unvalidated. Grand Lake and other unresolved historical geometry were not forced into the fit.
- Rounded cape and lake endpoints carry 12 px working source uncertainty; changed or ambiguous harbour edges and spatial correlation limit interpretation.
