# Sheet 024 — Western Tobeatic lakes and provisional Rossignol frame

**RMS 155.19 m exceeds 100 m; median, P95 and maximum also fail their 75/150/200 m criteria.** 7 physical controls define the frozen similarity; 8 excluded checks give median 83.10 m, empirical P95 285.43 m, maximum 342.06 m. Bias: +1.68 m east, -6.63 m north. Distances are WGS84 geodesic ground metres. The active fit was frozen before its reported check scoring.

Original 5717 × 3934, 150 ppi JPEG extracted from https://novascotia.ca/natr/land/indexmaps/024.pdf. All source coordinates remain in the original frame. The separately accessible complete scan retains marginal attribution and warning.

The full mapped frame retains all Rossignol islands, connected lake reaches, western inland lakes and southern extensions. Seven controls and eight checks support only the narrow western strip; the large eastern lake complex is retained as unvalidated extrapolation.

Seven controls and eight excluded checks were frozen before the first constrained similarity fit and score. Physical review covered the whole map and seventeen detail pairs; points were adopted only where shared geometry was identifiable.

Lake Martin is a widening of Jordan River coded WARV20 feature 174699. The initial lake-only control selected a neighbouring body, and the lake-only check failed the distance guard. Both were rejected before fitting and the correct bank geometry was inspected.

The source includes nested island contours and a note about the 279-foot contour. These are not assumed to represent the current shoreline. No Bear/Coombs or eastern lake-complex match was forced.

The eastern sheet 032 discovery guide and direct south-adjacent sheet 025 guide are retained. Grays northern source and Beartrack southern reference were refined from native physical imagery before fitting.

Limitations:

- All adopted observations are in the western strip. Roughly the eastern two-thirds, including the Rossignol lake/island complex, remain independently unvalidated.
- Seven checks share control waterbodies; First Porcupine provides one separate nearby lake. Independence is limited by shared historical cartography.
- Bear/Coombs islands, Cataract/Mills lake connections and other Rossignol outlines differ substantially from modern water geometry. Neither old nested contours nor red boundary lines were treated as modern shorelines.
- Generalized lake tips, river-connected shore corners and narrow reaches carry 24 px working source uncertainty.
- The full map is retained under a constrained similarity; numerical statistics do not establish geographic accuracy in the extrapolated eastern area.
