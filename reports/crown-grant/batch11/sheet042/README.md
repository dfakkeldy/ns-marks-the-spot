Complete mapped frame retains Advocate Bay and Harbour, Cape d Or, Horseshoe Cove, Cape Spencer, Spencer Island, Parrsboro Shore, East Brookville, Brookville, Wards Brook, Greville Bay and the entire depicted Minas Channel. The full rectangular Isle Haute inset is separately transformed with its original water margin and graticule annotations.

Both components use constrained similarities chosen before scoring. Main controls are nearly collinear, so an unconstrained affine was not justified. The Cape Spencer corner was refined from native source/reference imagery before first fitting.

Advocate harbour spits and annotation-obscured northern/eastern stream mouths were not forced into precise correspondences. Horseshoe Cove supplies a separate mainland check, while three other checks pair with control capes/island.

Printed inset longitude65 degrees and latitude45 degrees15 minutes located Isle Haute provisionally. No printed graticule coordinate or adjacent-sheet prediction entered either fit. The initial main-frame reference query excluded the island and was preserved before expanding westward.

Inset southern control was refined to the southern tip. The first southwest check proposal landed on a separate offshore islet; native audit rejected it and selected the matched main-island corner before scoring. Two checks remain, with all raw proposals preserved.

Identical inset and main-hole masks partition the full scan without omitted or duplicated source pixels. The broad Minas Channel water area remains retained despite its lack of independent checks.

Limitations:

- Inset median 87.38 m exceeds the 75 m companion bound despite RMS 88.07 m passing 100 m; only two checks.
- Main four checks and three controls are restricted to the Cape d Or / Cape Spencer / Spencer Island coastal cluster. Northern and eastern coasts and interior are unvalidated; no whole-sheet accuracy claim.
- Inset only two checks sample one small island; sparse empirical statistics do not validate the whole island or establish regional accuracy.
- Main source uncertainty 18 px; inset 30 px because of its strongly generalized shore.
- No geographic or user acceptance is implied by numerical passes or completion.

Both output-alpha coverage and the union of native masks pass, with no omitted/excess content outside edge tolerances and no source overlap. Each frozen transform retains the original pixel frame.
