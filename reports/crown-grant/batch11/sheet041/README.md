Complete mapped frame retains Apple River, Pudsey and Sword Points, Apple Head, Squally and Green Points, Sand River, New Salem, Howard Lake, Refugee Cove and Advocate. The complete rectangular southwest continuation inset retains Cape Chignecto and all its depicted coastal land and Bay of Fundy water; its transform is independent from the main map.

Main affine uses six controls and five checks reserved before fitting. All adopted main points were inspected in broad context and native crosshair views; dashed grant-to-low-water boundaries were distinguished from the physical shores.

Inset constrained similarity uses three controls and four reserved checks. Four corner/tip proposals were corrected from native imagery before its first fit and score; raw proposals remain preserved.

No printed inset scale or north arrow was identified. A provisional same-orientation seam translation located the coast only; neither the seam nor a guide prediction entered either fit.

The main-hole and inset rings are identical and include the full inset border. No source pixel is duplicated or omitted by the partition; all other map land and water remains in the main component.

Limitations:

- Main checks cover northern/western shores and Howard Lake; most interior, southern coast and east margin lack independent validation.
- Inset checks all follow one short coastal strip; no inland validation and no claim of independent regional sampling.
- Source uncertainty 18 px. Thick generalized shores and overlapping annotations limit point precision.
- Numerical passes and assessment completion do not establish geographic or user acceptance.

Both output-alpha coverage and the union of native masks pass, with no omitted/excess content outside edge tolerances and no source overlap. Each frozen transform retains the original pixel frame.
