# Sheet 052 — Cape Blomidon and Minas Basin

**RMS 141.32 m exceeds 100 m; regional limitations remain.** 4 physical controls define the frozen similarity; 4 checks excluded from fitting give median 101.66 m, empirical P95 220.49 m, maximum 238.04 m. Bias: -9.94 m east, +61.49 m north. Distances are WGS84 geodesic ground metres. The active fit was frozen before its reported check scoring.

Original 5663 × 3848, 150 ppi JPEG extracted from https://novascotia.ca/natr/land/indexmaps/052.pdf. All source coordinates remain in the original frame. The separately accessible complete scan retains marginal attribution and warning.

Complete original mapped frame retains Scotsman Bay, Cape Blomidon, Lower Blomidon, Delhaven, Pereau, Medford, Kingsport, Longspell Point, the partial Long Island and Boot Island depictions, Split Rock, Bramber, Starratt Point, Cambridge, Pembroke and the entire original Minas Basin water area. The large grant list remains in the raster; no internal crop or inset is introduced.

A constrained similarity was selected before scoring to avoid unsupported affine shear across the ocean and northern coast. Longspell and Medford source crosshairs were corrected onto the shore before the first fit; raw proposals remain preserved.

All observations were independently matched against NSTDB water geometry. The adjacent-sheet matrix supplied only the discovery frame.

The original full mapped frame is retained even where controls and checks provide no geographic validation.

Limitations:

- RMS, median, P95 and maximum exceed their respective 100/75/150/200 m bounds; the first transform and all errors remain unchanged.
- Four controls lie in the southern coastal band and Kingsport. Northern Scotsman Bay, Cape Blomidon and most open water lack independent validation.
- Four checks include paired Split Rock/Pembroke segments plus separate Medford and Evangeline coast regions; sample remains small and spatially limited.
- Source uncertainty 18 px; broad generalized curves were not forced into precise point matches.
- Paddy Island has no unambiguous matching island outline in the downloaded reference. Its historical depiction remains retained, with no cause asserted.
- Numerical assessment does not establish geographic or user acceptance.

The actual private raster is 6073 × 5192. Output alpha passes the independent polygon comparison with zero missing/excess cells outside the one-output-cell edge tolerance. Actual browser verification is recorded in `browser-verification.json`. This is geographic research imagery, with no public scan upload or production activation.

Source/reference/fit/raster hashes, all proposal packets and rejected matches are retained. Raw imagery, paired native crosshairs and vector extracts remain in `.crown-grant-local/`. Canonical fit/check files identify adopted points; raw proposals are not additional controls or checks.
