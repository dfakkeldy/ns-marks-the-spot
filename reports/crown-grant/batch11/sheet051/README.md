No inset or internal crop is introduced.

Generalized Cranberry/Jeffers main lake configurations and Clarke Head were not forced into matches. The adopted inland features are Leeks Lake, Summit Lake and the small pond west of Cranberry Lake.

The adjacent-sheet transform supplied only a discovery guide. All adopted observations were independently matched to modern NSTDB vertices in the original JPEG frame.

Limitations:

- Five checks share control lakes or islands; Cape Sharp and the smaller Two Brothers island provide separate feature checks.
- Northwestern mainland and much northern interior lack independent validation; water-area retention does not establish positional accuracy there.
- Source uncertainty 18 px; historical shoreline generalization remains.
- Cape Split control is the mainland tip. Separate modern offshore stacks are not assumed to be represented by the historical cape outline.
- Numerical passes do not establish geographic or user acceptance.
