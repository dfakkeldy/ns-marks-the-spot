Limitations:

- Five excluded checks give RMS 59.80 m, median 36.82 m, P95 103.61 m and maximum 118.07 m. Numerical bounds pass, but five observations in two clusters do not validate the whole sheet.
- Controls and checks are concentrated on the northwestern coast and southeastern Harbour Centre shore. Pond and island endpoint pairs and nearby coastal mouths are spatially correlated.
- The broad central, western and southwestern interior and the northeastern inland region remain geographically unvalidated. Ambiguous parcel/stream intersections were not forced into observations; all original content remains visible.
- A constrained similarity was chosen before fitting to limit unsupported affine shear across the sparse control layout. It cannot establish local map accuracy in the unsampled interior. Generalized coastal controls/checks use 18 px source uncertainty; pond/island points use 12 px.
