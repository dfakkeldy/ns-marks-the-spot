Limitations:

- Four excluded checks give RMS 96.76 m, within the 100 m ceiling, but P95 151.55 m exceeds the 150 m companion bound. Median is 68.78 m and maximum 164.18 m. The first result remains qualified.
- Every adopted control/check lies in the southeastern harbour cluster. The western interior, northern half and northeastern Caribou coast have no accepted independent validation. This fit does not establish whole-sheet geographic accuracy.
- Two checks are opposite endpoints of the tiny Lochbroom and Ballast islets used for controls. They are strongly spatially correlated; four observations are not four independent regional samples.
- Generalized shore/headland observations use 18 px working source uncertainty. The modern causeway, changed barriers, marshes and engineered shores remain visible but unvalidated.
