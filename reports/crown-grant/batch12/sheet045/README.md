**RMS 66.49 m passes 100 m; regional limitations remain.** 8 physical controls define the frozen affine; 9 excluded checks give median 40.55 m, empirical P95 120.40 m, maximum 150.91 m. Bias: -13.18 m east, +14.67 m north. Distances are WGS84 geodesic ground metres. The active fit was frozen before its reported check scoring.

Complete mapped frame retains Aldersville, New Ross, New Russell, Leminster (as printed), Benjamin Mills, Vaughan and Upper Vaughan, Caribou or Long Lake, Salmon Tail lakes, Porcupine Lake, Duck Pond, Five Island Lake, Little Salter and Bill lakes, Lawson and Darling lakes, the Otter lakes and the depicted Avon and Gold River systems. Every mapped land and water area is retained; no inset or internal crop.

Eight controls and nine checks were assigned before the first affine fit. Broad lake shapes, adjoining streams and native source/reference crosshairs were inspected for all adopted observations.

The Little Salter control was refined to its eastern tip before fitting. The original proposal is preserved. The printed names Porcupine and Bill are used; a preliminary panther discovery filename does not name an adopted feature.

Bennett and Kerr/ Round Lake candidates were not forced into unresolved modern matches. Lawson and Caribou observations retain explicit inlet/outlet or shore-tip definitions rather than adopting their labels as coordinates.

The original complete frame is retained irrespective of the check coverage. No post-score changes are applied to observations or the fitted transform.

Limitations:

- Eight of nine checks pair with control lakes; only Little Otter is a separate lake check. These samples are spatially correlated and do not establish whole-sheet accuracy.
- The heavily annotated western interior and some river reaches lack independent validation.
- Source uncertainty 18 px; small lake outlines, outlets and inlets are generalized.
- Assessment completion and any numerical pass do not establish geographic or user acceptance.
