Six controls and six checks were frozen before first fitting. A northern Mill Brook control gives support away from the diagonal lake cluster. Its Moore Brook check reference was refined to the actual confluence before scoring.

The initial neighbouring-sheet guide cut off corresponding islands in detail crops. A rough translation established from a distinctive eastern Gaspereau island improved discovery; neither guide coordinates nor that rough anchor entered the fit. Both guides and all initial views are retained.

The southern Gaspereau island, western River Lake island and southern Morton candidate were not adopted because the modern outlines have different connectivity. Both Black River proposals were rejected after native audit exposed wrong type-filtered reference selections and ambiguous changed mouth geometry.

The five adopted islands were identified using their surrounding island groups and shoreline context, then inspected with native source and reference crosshairs. Full map coverage remains independent from the geographic support of these observations.

Limitations:

- Five checks pair with control islands; the sixth samples a separate Mill Brook tributary junction. These are correlated drainage/island samples, not independent regional validation.
- Eastern third and northern corners lack independent checks.
- Lake outlines are generalized and some nearby historical islands have different modern connectivity. Only matched features were adopted; no water-level or change-cause conclusion is asserted.
- Source uncertainty 24 px for lake islands and 18 px for stream junctions; the fit is unweighted.
- Assessment completion and any numerical pass do not establish geographic or user acceptance.
