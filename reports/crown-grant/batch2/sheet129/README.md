Limitations:

- Main checks are regional lake cohorts; eight checks remain below the distributed target and companion median/P95/maximum bounds fail
- Northern Gisborne, Wreck Cove and French Lake outlines differ markedly from present flowages; those areas and far western river valleys lack direct independent validation
- Inset uses two physical coastal controls and three excluded checks along one narrow coast; near-zero control residual is not proof of accuracy
- Coastal notches, stream mouths and shoulders are generalized; working source uncertainty is larger than for sharp lake corners

Both output-alpha coverage and the union of native masks pass, with no omitted/excess content outside edge tolerances and no source overlap. Each frozen transform retains the original pixel frame.
