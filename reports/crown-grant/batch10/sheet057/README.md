Original source: https://novascotia.ca/natr/land/indexmaps/057.pdf, 5699 × 3850, 150 ppi. The complete original scan is separately accessible with marginal attribution and warning.

Complete original mapped frame retains Prospect, Whites Lake, Terence Bay, Pennant, Sambro, Ketch Harbour, Chebucto Head, Herring Cove, all depicted offshore islands and the full Atlantic Ocean area. The rectangular Peggy Cove, Dover, Indian Island and partial Taylor Island inset is independently transformed using a complementary native mask.

Main seven controls and seven checks were assigned before first fitting. Betty Island southern source and the two pond outlet references were corrected from native imagery before scoring; original proposals are preserved.

Inset similarity was chosen before scoring because its three controls follow a narrow diagonal coast. Peggy Point, Dover and Taylor extrema and Indian Island and Corney Rock check points were refined before its first fit. The generalized Peggy Point southern shore check was rejected before scoring.

The NRCan previously official Peggy Point name record supplied only a coarse discovery location. All adopted coordinates come from independently matched NSTDB water vertices. No name coordinate entered either fit.

The inset mask encloses its full original border, including the ocean and clipped Taylor Island depiction. Identical main-hole and inset rings partition the scan without duplication. The I.S.57B callout refers to a separate sheet and is not another inset.

Limitations:

- Main seven checks pass the 100 m RMS ceiling but all are paired with control islands or ponds; the interior, most mainland coastline and northern edge lack independent validation.
- Inset three checks are on only Indian Island and Corney Rock. Its narrow coastal sample does not validate mainland interiors or the northern and southeastern edges.
- Source uncertainty 18 px in both components. Small generalized outlines and annotation overlap limit point precision.
- Assessment completion and numerical passes do not establish geographic or user acceptance.

Both output-alpha coverage and the union of native masks pass, with no omitted/excess content outside edge tolerances and no source overlap. Each frozen transform retains the original pixel frame. Browser proof is recorded separately. Source scans, references, warps and paired audit images remain private; public records contain coordinates, code, hashes, measurements and provenance.
