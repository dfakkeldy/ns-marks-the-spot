The entire mapped frame is partitioned into the main coast/open-water map and the displaced Prim Point–Culloden/Victoria inset. Both use original scan pixels and separate similarities. All water, islands and edge extensions are retained; large unsampled areas remain provisional.

Main five controls/five checks and inset three controls/four checks were frozen before the first component fits and scores. Main ten native crosshairs, inset seven crosshairs and the corrected Delap bank were inspected.

The inset is physically displaced above sheet 013. Its paper-offset discovery guide was refined from the Prim Point coastline before adopting any inset points. Original and refined guides are retained.

The full outer rectangle includes a narrow scan-border allowance. Main has a concave boundary and inset exactly fills its upper-left cutout, with shared seam at source x2940/y1312. Native corner detail confirms the printed boundary.

A decorative letter S was rejected as a possible pond; harbour structures and the Prim light symbol were not adopted as natural shore controls.

Limitations:

- Main observations occupy the narrow southern coastal strip and three small inland ponds. Broad open water and most intervening interior remain independently unvalidated.
- Main checks include two control ponds, a nearby Young Cove stream mouth, independent Delap Cove and a separate far-eastern pond. Shared historical cartography and nearby coast features limit independence.
- Inset observations are confined to the coast. Prim and Caldwell checks are close to control headlands; the broad ocean interior is not independently validated.
- Thin generalized coasts, stream-mouth bank junctions, small ponds and shoreline change carry 24 px working source uncertainty.
- Main and inset have independent transforms and scores; no pooled accuracy claim is made.

Both output-alpha coverage and the union of native masks pass, with no omitted/excess content outside edge tolerances and no source overlap. Each frozen transform retains the original pixel frame.
