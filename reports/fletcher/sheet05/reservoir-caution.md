# Modern reservoir context

The historical Chéticamp headwaters differ substantially from the large modern water polygon in the southern interior. No modern reservoir shore is used as a control or check.

[Nova Scotia Power dates Wreck Cove construction to 1975–1978](https://www.nspower.ca/cleanandgreen/clean-energy/clean-energy-sources/wreck-cove), after Fletcher's survey. Its [2018 Hydro Asset Study, section 3.16](https://www.nspower.ca/docs/default-source/irp/20181221-ns-power-hydro-asset-study-redacted.pdf?sfvrsn=a8dabcf6_1) identifies Chéticamp Flowage among the waters supplying the Wreck Cove system. These primary records support treating modern impounded water geometry separately from historical physical matching; they do not establish a precise former shoreline or validate an individual correspondence.
