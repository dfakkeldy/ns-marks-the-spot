# Inverness north — correct First Fork placement, retain a failed fresh check

A broad river-network audit identifies a source-placement error in I10. Its previous pixel (7347, 25733) lies on the downstream river/road complex. Continuous tracing from the north main river, MacLeods Pool and eastern First Fork locates the actual north-bank apex at **(7948, 25473)**. The original modern bank coordinate, feature 182427 vertex 0, is unchanged. `I10-correction.json` and the enlarged crosshair preserve the correction and source frame. All original observations, four-control trials and results remain byte-preserved in the prior report.

With the other three controls unchanged, the corrected affine improves the same retained Pleasant Bay N03 diagnostic from **167.24 m to 125.73 m**. This is one diagnostic, not fresh or whole-panel validation. Its full distribution and bias are in `correction-comparison.json`. The corrected four-control fit was frozen before selecting Second Fork Brook I13.

The new I13 bank-entry check is **353.06 m** away (116.67 m west / 333.23 m south). For n=1, RMS, median, empirical P95 and maximum all equal that discrepancy. It lies outside the source control hull and shares the southern river region with I10; 100 m stated observation uncertainty is retained, not subtracted. Native tracing follows the long eastern Second Fork branch, fall, southern tributary and paired main-river bends. Modern named Second Fork Brook 277200 meets bank segments 183240/183241 at the measured point. `fresh-validation-review.csv` contains only the frozen controls and this fresh check. No tuning followed the failure.

I12 has a separate correspondence problem. Its old source pixel is the first northern side tributary east of First Fork, while its modern coordinate is a junction with a substantial southern branch and northeast/east continuation. The historical Moises Brook split is farther east. Their original claimed identity is not established. `I12-topology-audit.json` withholds the pair from valid post-correction checks, without changing or erasing its original **1813.63 m** result, snapping its coordinates, or promoting it. That old discrepancy cannot be attributed entirely to sheet distortion.

Retain the corrected affine as the best provisional physical fit; it still fails the current 250 m objective and lacks northern, intervening interior and edge support. The northern coastline context still shows repeated small falls and a generalized Lowland Cove mouth; this pass adds no defensible northern point. No usable raster, full-content render, seam, browser or geographic acceptance is claimed. The nonsingular affine cannot fold, but that does not establish geographic support.
