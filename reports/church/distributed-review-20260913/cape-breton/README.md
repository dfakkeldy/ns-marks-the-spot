For n=1, RMS, median, empirical P95 and maximum all equal 522.63 m. Hay lies outside the source hull and close to Scatarie; it does not validate either mainland control. Three controls provide no redundancy. No tuning followed; preserve this first result as a rejected/provisional experiment.

The [NRCan Scotch Lake record](https://geonames.nrcan.gc.ca/search-place-names/unique?id=CBHPK) corroborates locality only. Its coordinate was not used as fitting evidence. The initial supporting-ID draft was corrected by exact feature-incidence review before scoring, with coordinates unchanged.
