F02, the verified northern diamond islet in Inhabitants Basin, was promoted from the preceding fresh set into the eighth control. Its original 325 m discrepancy remains in refinement-02. Its coordinates were not changed. The remaining nine preceding checks became diagnostic; on that identical set TPS RMS improved from 158.5 m to 126.9 m. F03 improved from 382 m to 234 m; F04 worsened from 169 m to 201 m.

The eight-control fit was frozen before six additional observations (G01–G06). Four northern/southern observations measured 117–201 m, while two eastern lakes measured 747 m and 831 m. The six-check result was 472.4 m RMS and failed the unchanged 400 m gate. This numerical stage was not rendered or adopted for publication.
