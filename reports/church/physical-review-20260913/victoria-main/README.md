# Victoria main: fix the longitude anchor, then test physical geography

The generated main-panel graticule was anchored **one 10-minute meridian too far east**. `longitude-label-native.jpg` shows 60°40′ at the southern neatline near native x=23,595, on the second detected meridian. The old mesh assigned that meridian 60°30′. `original-graticule.csv` preserves it byte-for-byte; the production anchor now attaches the engraved 60°40′ value to lattice index 1, and the generated CSV was regenerated. No latitude or source-pixel coordinates changed. A regression test tied to that printed label failed before correction and passes afterward; all 428 Church tests pass.

On the identical four physical checks, the original graticule TPS measures **13,285.29 m RMS**; the corrected graticule TPS measures **784.14 m RMS**. This is a real anchor-indexing fix, not a datum shift estimated from checks. The source label independently establishes the longitude correction. Historical July reports and their rejected status remain untouched.

Four physical controls were then measured: Kidston Island, Hume Island, northeastern Bird Island/Ciboux, and the eastern Baddeck Lake basin. Four independently reserved features were excluded: southwestern Bird Island/Hertford, McLeod Lake, Bone Island and Stony Island. The first physical affine trial scores **603.46 m RMS**, median **514.68 m**, empirical P95 **891.59 m**, maximum **931.12 m**, with mean residual **138.82 m east / 213.87 m south**. TPS scores 615.82 m RMS. Neither meets the ≤250 m working or ≤200 m stretch target, and four checks cannot meet the historical six-check gate.

McLeod Lake is the largest discrepancy. A bounded fifth-control trial promoted its unchanged, physically identified centroid and preserved its first failed check. On the exact same three remaining diagnostics, the four-control affine scores 443.36 m RMS, five-control affine 480.48 m, and five-control TPS 512.00 m. The additional-control trials are rejected; retain the four-control affine only as a failed/provisional experiment. All checks are diagnostic after these comparisons. There are zero fresh validation checks after refinement and no accepted delivered raster from this pass.

The correspondence figures distinguish the offshore Bird Islands, southern St Patricks Channel islands and the interior Baddeck/McLeod basin region. Hertford is spatially correlated with the Ciboux control; Bone and Stony occupy the same southern channel. The northern main-panel extension, broad mountainous interior, southeast shoreline and panel seam remain unvalidated. The historical Christmas Island shoreline closure and French/Loon lake-network correspondence were not resolved well enough for a control; they were not measured or snapped to predictions. The mapped-content boundary still needs a full review of western extensions before a whole-panel delivery. No full-panel rendered, distortion, seam, browser or geographic acceptance is asserted for failed trials.
