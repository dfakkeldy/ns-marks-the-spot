# Cape Breton: identified offshore features, mainland correspondences unresolved

Scatarie Island's complete outer coastline and nearby Hay Island now have measured, editable physical observations, source-frame receipts, reviewed crosshairs and original NSTDB rings/IDs. Scatarie is a prospective control; Hay is reserved as a check. Both occupy the same eastern offshore group. **One control is insufficient to fit a two-dimensional panel**, so RMS, median, P95, maximum and directional bias are all unavailable, not zero. No warp was produced and neither working target is established.

The full Scatarie outline follows the innermost land/water line, excluding concentric offshore engraving lines, interior ponds and detached rocks. Its displayed outline is converted explicitly from the 4,800 × 3,300 native crop to the 1,600 × 1,100 review frame; the centroid was checked again on a direct 800 × 800 native crop. Hay was traced at native resolution. Their full modern rings are plotted in direct geographic aspect separately from the search guide. Shape generalization remains approximately 170–250 m observation uncertainty, retained rather than subtracted from an accuracy score.

A 30,864-feature original NSTDB water-line extract fills the previously missing mainland/eastern reference coverage (`reference-receipt.json`). The rough search bootstrap used approximate locality positions only; it was not accepted, scored, rendered or used as a physical observation. The numbered township mesh remains unsupported as a geographic graticule.

Two mainland candidates were investigated and deliberately withheld:

- `CB02`: the historical Pottle Lake vicinity depicts multiple basins, while the modern geometry includes an extensive reservoir and engineered closures. The same whole-basin definition was not established. [CBRM's source-water protection material](https://cbrm.ns.ca/home-property/water-wastewater/water-services/source-water-protection/) identifies Pottle Lake as the Northside water source; the [provincial watershed description](https://novascotia.ca/just/regulations/regs/envpottlelake.htm) explicitly refers to the reservoir. Neither source proves a historical shoreline unchanged.
- `CB03`: the engraved inland lake near Eskasoni, labelled approximately “Sanacadie,” has candidate modern basins with conflicting relative size/spacing and incomplete identity evidence. A nearby-looking polygon or historical label alone cannot select one. No centroid from this unresolved pair enters a fit.

The [official Scatarie Island record](https://geonames.nrcan.gc.ca/search-place-names/unique?id=CBHMU) corroborates the named island, but its label/locality coordinate was not used as the control coordinate; the measured NSTDB whole-island ring supplies that centroid. The exact scan is the preserved 36,223 × 35,027 source with SHA recorded in `observations.json`. Source-derived figures retain Rumsey/Stanford provenance and recorded CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 terms.

Retain the previously repaired content cutline, including southern mapped extensions and excluding Sydney Harbour and other independent plans. Mainland northern, western, interior, southern and edge controls/checks remain unresolved; no full-panel geographic, rendered, distortion, alpha, seam or browser acceptance is claimed. No tiles, catalog activation or deployment. The bounded correspondence attempt is preserved and work continues to the Inverness south baseline audit.
