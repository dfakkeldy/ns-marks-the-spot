# Inverness north physical trial

A four-control physical affine trial is preserved in `physical-trial.csv`, with TPS as a recorded comparison. Polletts Cove N02 and Fishing Cove N04 are promoted unchanged from the preserved September 12 failed diagnostic set; every original graticule result remains intact. Native/broad correspondence review adds First Fork on the Northeast Margaree River (I10) and the southern terminal lobe of Cheticamp Island (I11). Controls span the coast and southern inland edge; the middle interior and northern extension remain weakly supported.

The retained Pleasant Bay estuary diagnostic N03 improves from 2,012.55 m to 167.24 m on the affine trial (TPS 283.49 m). For this **one diagnostic**, RMS/median/empirical P95/maximum are all 167.24 m, mean residual 115.62 m east / 120.85 m north. A single migrating outlet does not establish the ≤250 m working target, the six-check historical gate, or panel acceptance.

After that fit was frozen, upstream First Fork junction I12 was traced and checked. It misses by **1,813.63 m**, with mean residual 1,699.26 m east / 633.86 m north. With only one new check, RMS/median/empirical P95/maximum all equal that discrepancy and carry no distribution claim. I12 is spatially correlated with I10; it adds evidence of local failure, not broad validation. The first result is preserved in `fresh-I12-score.json`; I12 is not promoted or snapped to the trial prediction.

The affine trial is **rejected for geographic use**. The coherent coastline improvement does not repair local southern interior distortion. Resolving the tributary network and obtaining additional distributed interior, northern-tip and coastal checks is required before a defensible full-panel warp. Lowland Cove's outlet definition and similar small coastal falls remain unresolved; no points were manufactured to reach a quota. This bounded trial is saved while work continues to Victoria.

No production GCP or boundary was replaced, no raster was delivered from this failed trial, and no rendered/geographic acceptance, browser validation, catalog activation or publication is claimed. A nonsingular affine fit cannot fold, but that mathematical fact does not establish geographic support. Adjacent-sheet physical seams remain untested.

`observations.json`, `candidate-I12.json`, paired native/geographic crosshairs and selected original NSTDB features preserve identities, native crop frames, roles and uncertainty. The direct geographic figures use 1,600 ground-metre windows, not the historical search warp. `physical_trial.py` reproduces the original four-control trials. Distances use the existing ground-metre scorer; P95 is empirical NumPy linear interpolation. Rumsey/Stanford source imagery retains recorded CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 terms; large originals remain outside Git.
