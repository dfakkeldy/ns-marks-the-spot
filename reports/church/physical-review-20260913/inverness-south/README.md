# Inverness south: accepted baseline preserved, separate physical refinement

The accepted July controls, eleven hand checks, artifact and 400/900/1500 m gates are unchanged. The historical record remains 333.29 m RMS; replay with the current 6,371,008.8 m sphere and linear P95 calculation gives **332.91 m RMS**, median 291.79 m, P95 465.35 m, maximum 467.74 m, mean residual 47.88 m west / 250.81 m north. The small RMS difference is the distance convention, and the historical P95 used a nearest-rank statistic. Neither historical acceptance nor its original numbers is revised. Both exceed the new 250 m working RMS target.

All eleven original pixels were revisited in native crops. The Margaree Island hand point is near the eastern shore rather than the outer-outline centroid; the earlier automatic audit had already noted this disagreement. Four islands were separately retraced at native resolution for a physical trial: Margaree, Indian in Whycocomagh Bay, Cameron, and the southwestern Crammond member. Their source polygons and new centroids are in `control-observations.json`. Modern coordinates remain the original measured island centroids. The original pixels/failed measurements remain preserved, and all four feature aliases are excluded from every new check set.

On the **same seven remaining historical diagnostics**, baseline TPS scores 303.60 m RMS and the physical affine scores **131.05 m RMS**, median **107.55 m**, empirical P95 **209.43 m**, maximum **223.71 m**, mean residual **13.44 m west / 76.84 m north**. Four-control TPS scores 128.60 m RMS but has a larger 256.25 m maximum. The affine was selected for its simpler model and smaller tail. Several retained approximate hand points, including IS06 near an engraved edge, still require a separate centroid audit before any new validation claim. These seven checks are diagnostics; their original approximate hand placement and strong southern clustering remain limitations.

The selected affine was frozen before measuring the **fresh Lake Ainslie outlet**. That one inland feature misses by **290.70 m**, compared with 388.52 m on the unchanged accepted baseline. For n=1, RMS, median, P95 and maximum all equal 290.70 m; mean residual is 166.85 m west / 238.05 m north. This fresh source point lies outside the four-control hull. The historical mouth is generalized and wider than the modern throat; the stated 180 m observation uncertainty remains in the measurement. No coordinate or control was changed to lower this fresh error. It exceeds the working target and does not establish panel-wide accuracy. `fresh-validation-review.csv` contains the identical frozen controls plus only that fresh check; never pool it with the seven diagnostics and call the result fresh validation.

Western Port Hood/Smith and Henry island contexts were inspected. Complete stable coast closures could not be established in the inspected engraving, especially beside the panel divider; no whole-island centroid was manufactured. Loch Ban is connected to Lake Ainslie, and the available whole-lake context did not cover a complete outer basin; no separate lake check was inferred. Interior mountains, western edges, northern inland regions and adjacent-panel seams still lack distributed independent validation. The 20–30 check ambition remains unmet. Preserve the best provisional affine and the accepted historical baseline separately.

## Actual raster and delivery

The review GeoTIFF is **4,757 × 6,466 pixels at 20 projected metres**, EPSG:3857, using the full existing south content cutline. It is a reduced-resolution research review, not a replacement for the accepted artifact. `final-artifact-receipt.json` records exact source, controls, output locations and SHA-256 `f3036573572ccadc3a50b87a9e13e82036d7ff3698a6ea4602cbd4fd03d4f176`.

The first GCP/order-1 render clipped a northern triangle, leaving 411,163 transparent interior cells. Encoding the unchanged frozen forward affine explicitly as a GeoTransform eliminates the gap: **zero holes across 22,718,783 expected interior cells**, with one output-cell boundary tolerance. The source, controls, cutline, cell size and bilinear resampling were unchanged. The underlying GDAL cause is not established. A separate native-level GCP probe was aborted after more than ten minutes; its partial output is not a completed experiment. Failed/aborted receipts are retained in `render-comparison.json` and linked files. `render_review.py` now uses the verified explicit affine path.

The affine determinant is negative and nonsingular; an affine transform cannot fold. This does not establish geographic accuracy. Actual-raster/reference windows in `warped-review/` show Margaree, Whycocomagh, Malagawatch and the Lake Ainslie outlet. They preserve visible local shape differences and the inland shift. The production web parser and affine solver match GDAL's forward calculations within 0.001 projected metre (`../parser-verification.json`). Embedded-raster browser verification confirms import, retained dimensions, enabled state after reload and rendering at zooms 9 and 12 with no captured console/page errors (`browser-verification.json`). The CSV belongs to the original 34,427 × 34,543 scan; the GeoTIFF carries its own georeferencing. no raw-scan browser mesh acceptance is implied.

`wrong-input-probe.json` discloses an initial audit-input mismatch: `drawn-south.json` contains only two automatically accepted shapes from an older experiment, not the accepted eleven-check set. That two-point score was discarded as baseline evidence. `historical-checks.csv` is the byte-preserved production eleven-check input and `baseline-replay.json` is its actual replay.

All source-derived figures retain David Rumsey Map Collection / Stanford Libraries provenance and recorded CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 scan terms. Modern reference geometry is original NSTDB data. No new geographic acceptance, tiles, activation, deployment or change to the July acceptance follows from this refinement.
