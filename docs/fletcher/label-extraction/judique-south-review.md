# Judique south to Long Point: third transcription batch

**23 new printed annotations bring the research inventory to 93.** This batch records five schools, three mill labels (two printed as Mills), two forges, a post office, a shop, four personal-name labels, three road names, a brook, two separate Long Point labels and a printed elevation annotation. Counts describe inscriptions, not independently established sites.

[Reviewed inventory](judique-south.json) · [Raw candidates and review trail](judique-south-workflow/README.md) · [Native source receipt](sheet-19-native-source-2026-09-05.json)

The core is **[2700, 3500, 3100, 2350]** in the original 10815 × 7549 sheet. Six overlapping windows extend the Judique coastal inventory south through Long Point and nearby inland roads. The core overlaps the earlier Judique area by 200 pixels in the vertical direction; no earlier annotation was duplicated. Three extra windows finish intersecting edge labels.

The union of the three batches’ core rectangles covers **22,865,000 pixels, about 28.01% of the sheet image**, including sea and blank areas. This is reviewed image area, not a percentage of labels extracted or proof of complete coverage. The rest of sheet 19 remains unfinished.

## What the assembly line handled

Three isolated Sol workers received source images and transcription instructions. The coordinator reviewed the whole windows, compared all 29 candidate excerpts against the source and inspected all 23 final label excerpts. Overlap deduplication and edge completion reduced 29 raw records to 22 annotations. The coordinator added the omitted 700 Fᵀ. annotation. Raw candidates retain their original errors and crop-local boxes; the reviewed inventory records corrections and whole-sheet rectangles.

The review removed an invented initial from A. MᶜDougall, widened clipped boxes and completed General Line Road and Wood Road from source pixels. Sol’s text output was useful here, but this does not establish unattended reliability or measured cost savings. The [earlier six-crop comparison](luna-pilot/README.md) is complete with Luna, Terra and Sol; the user chose to set aside the optional Claude test.

## Remaining work

- Widely spaced C / R / A lettering in the eastern window remains deferred until the complete inscription can be inspected. This does not silently classify it as geology.
- Continue the south/east source windows and then the rest of sheet 19. Edge-context windows do not count as fully inventoried.
- Trace road and watercourse connections and associate printed symbols before investigating modern placement. These additions have no site coordinates or modern name matches.
- Keep Chisholm Brook distinct from Rory Chisholm’s Brook in the earlier Judique batch. Similar personal names and repeated generic labels do not establish a common feature.
