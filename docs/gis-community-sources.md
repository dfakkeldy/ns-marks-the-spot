It is a viewing area, not a project boundary. All six mapped sources are disabled until selected and retain their own publisher, date, licence and caveat in the map catalogue.

The Nova Scotia catalogue datasets carry the [Open Government Licence – Nova Scotia](https://novascotia.ca/opendata/licence.asp). Licence was checked in each dataset's `/api/views/{id}.json` metadata, rather than inferred from the presence of a public API. The NRCan dataset's [catalogue](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/79fdad93-9025-49ad-ba16-c26d718cc070) identifies the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada). The rendered map should be treated as research context, not as surveyed or current site evidence.

## Researched sources held back

- **Canadian Aquatic Barriers Database:** The live [CABD API](https://cabd-web.azurewebsites.net/cabd-api/features?bbox=-61.52%2C45.69%2C-61.28%2C45.90&crs=EPSG%3A4326) returned 442 features in the viewing box (435 stream crossings, six waterfalls, one dam), with 385 `Unknown` passability statuses. Its response metadata declared `data_licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/`, including separate dam and waterfall responses. The [CABD overview](https://cabd-docs.netlify.app/docs_user/cabd_overview) instead states CC BY-SA 4.0. The live response's more specific non-commercial licence and this conflict prevent a clear grant for the business-hosted public map. Do not display these features until the publisher resolves the licence; if added later, retain `Unknown` and other passability classes exactly.
- **[Geoheritage Sites of Nova Scotia, DP ME 477](https://novascotia.ca/natr/meb/download/dp477.asp):** 2017 data is delivered as downloadable SHP/GDB/KML, with a [product-specific licence](https://novascotia.ca/natr/meb/download/dp477dll.asp) that requires the licence to accompany redistributed data. No published live GeoJSON/REST endpoint, converted map asset or local coverage check was established in this batch. The product page also asks visitors to obtain landowner permission.
- **Inverness wind zoning:** The [official wind map](https://edpc.ca/plandocs/inwind/IN_Wind_Zoning.pdf) is a separate planning product. No authoritative versioned wind-zone GIS feed or reuse terms were confirmed. General zoning must not stand in for wind zoning, and a PDF trace would not establish an authoritative boundary.
