Privacy Policy

NS Marks The Spot

Last updated August 30, 2026.

Summary

NS Marks The Spot does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases. Location access is optional. On the native app it is used for map display when you choose to enable the Current Location control. On the browser map you can also mark a point or save a track locally on the device.

Location

If you grant location permission, the native app uses your device location to show where you are on the map and to center the map on your current position. The native app does not store your location history, link location to your identity, sell it, or use it for tracking.

The native app is built with Apple's MapKit. Apple map services and iOS may process map and location requests as part of providing the system map experience.

On the browser map, location is also optional and stays on your device. A live watch can show your position without saving it. If you mark a point or save a track recording, those geometries — and for a saved recording, the raw GPX original — are stored locally as your user layers. They are not uploaded by NS Marks The Spot. They leave the device only if you export or share them yourself. Continuous live watching does not send your coordinates to an application server. If you attach a photo that includes a geotag, that location is used only if you accept the optional offer to move the feature to the photo's location.

Map And Tile Requests

The app displays OpenStreetMap and Apple basemaps, Hugh Fletcher historical overlay tiles, and Nova Scotia provincial geographic services. Those map providers may receive standard network requests for the tiles or layers you view. Searching by PID or civic address sends the search text to the provincial services that answer it. If you turn on the optional live-conditions layers in the browser map, your browser also requests camera imagery from 511 Nova Scotia and radar tiles from Environment and Climate Change Canada's MSC GeoMet. NS Marks The Spot does not operate its own tracking server.

Saved Areas And Cache

Viewed map tiles and prepared Fletcher saved areas are stored on your device to make the map more useful in weak-signal areas. You can clear cached data in the app. This offline data is not uploaded by NS Marks The Spot.

Your Own Maps And Drawings

Maps you import, the placements you give them, and the layers you draw are stored only on your device. NS Marks The Spot does not upload them, and they leave the device only when you export or share something yourself through the iOS share sheet.

Photos you attach to features on the browser map are stored only on your device. They are re-encoded so EXIF (including GPS) is stripped from stored and exported bytes, and they leave the device only if you export or share them yourself.

Bundled Tax-Sale Data

Municipal tax-sale notices ship inside the app as datasets built from public municipal records. They describe properties, not you: the app does not ingest assessed-owner names, and browsing the notices sends nothing about you to the municipalities.
