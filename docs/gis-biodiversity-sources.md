# Biodiversity overlays: source and verification receipt

Checked 27 September 2026. These overlays screen published geographic evidence; they do not establish a species occurrence, site condition, parcel overlap, or an access or regulatory decision. Each layer keeps its publisher, date, feature identity, and licence in the map metadata. A service error, empty returned view, and unmapped or withheld habitat remain different states.

### Core-habitat build receipt

The source is the original provincewide [FileGDB ZIP from entry 153](https://nsgi.novascotia.ca/WSF_DDS/DDS.svc/DownloadFile?tkey=kNNpTdP4QuNRSYtt&id=113219) (the direct token may change; discover it through the GDD). GeoNOVA's download dialog loads the unrestricted data licence for this entry even though the ListData API's `license_txt_url` field is blank. The ZIP includes the province's 15 December 2025 data description and dictionary. Its SHA-256 is `d0998a355e1f8658c1bf572f65723e809ec4577595e0ee051b73c0f59444b878`.

Run `python3 web/scripts/buildCoreHabitatSnapshot.py /path/to/NS_CoreHabitat.zip` from the repository root with GDAL `ogr2ogr` installed. The script rejects a different ZIP hash, selects only `NS_CoreHabitat_Poly` (3,660 features), retains source `OBJECTID`, names, taxon, year, source, recovery-document URL, status, population and source area, simplifies in native UTM 20 coordinates by **20 metres for display**, converts to 2D WGS84, and converts the one GDAL geometry collection to its polygon parts. It excludes the 99 public points; the source archive itself withholds location-sensitive Blanding's Turtle, Wood Turtle and Black Ash habitat. No withheld coordinates are reconstructed. The output is [ns-core-habitat.geojson](../web/public/ecology/ns-core-habitat.geojson): 4,564,818 bytes, SHA-256 `b3bfdde302054122e2a3de6ab715a4435aed15370d026f140b600a2bf4c93d61`. Geometry is display-only; the published FileGDB and recovery documents remain the source for analysis. The snapshot includes the licence's reproduction notice and accuracy disclaimer; the layer metadata carries its product attribution.

### Live-query receipt

For the Rhodena wider screening rectangle (W −61.52, S 45.69, E −61.28, N 45.90), `returnCountOnly` against the listed REST layers on 27 September 2026 returned ECCC final areas **3**, ECCC proposed areas **0**, DFO aquatic critical habitat **0**, DFO aquatic ranges **15**, NatureServe EBAR generalized ranges filtered to NS **226**, ELC districts **14**, ELC sections **301**, and DFO eelgrass sample points **0**. The core FileGDB native-UTM envelope screening returned **9 polygon features** and **0 points**. These are rectangle intersections, not intersections with a turbine, road or transmission alignment. The zero results are returned-empty for those particular source queries, not evidence of ecological absence. Service counts can change.

CNWI's REST feature query returns ArcGIS 400, “Requested operation is not supported by this service,” despite metadata advertising query. Its **image** path works: `MapServer/export` with `layers=show:1`, WGS84 bbox W −61.41, S 45.775, E −61.39, N 45.795 and an 800 × 800 transparent PNG returned visibly classified wetland polygons at about 1:10,500. The publisher's REST legend and `drawingInfo` agree on eight classes and colors. The wider Rhodena export was about 1:126,000, above layer 1's detail threshold, and returned transparent; REST layer 0 showed its coverage polygon at that scale. The [WMS GetCapabilities](https://maps-cartes.ec.gc.ca/arcgis/services/CWS_SCF/CNWI/MapServer/WMSServer?service=WMS&request=GetCapabilities) advertises the same classified layer as WMS name `0` (its WMS numbering differs from REST); a `GetMap` for the detail bbox returned nontransparent classified polygons. The application uses REST export at zoom 13 and higher, with `show:1`, avoiding the scale-dependent coverage surrogate. The federal catalog offers the English CNWI and French INTHC service separately; these are language variants of one inventory, not two sources.

ECCC's live field domains on `CriticalHabitat/MapServer/3` code `SARA_Status` as 0 null, 1 Extirpated, 2 Endangered, 3 Threatened, 4 Special Concern, 5 No Status, 6 Not at Risk. `RD_Status` is 0 null, 1 Posted Final, 2 Posted Proposed, 3 Not Yet Posted - CH Draft, 4 Not Yet Posted - CH Candidate, 5 Archived. Popup consumers should show these labels while retaining the source codes.

## Researched but not enabled

- **Nova Scotia Significant Species and Habitats**: The [public page](https://novascotia.ca/natr/wildlife/habitats/hab-data/) offers a 2024-08-19 generalized provincial shapefile; its [policy](https://novascotia.ca/natr/wildlife/pdf/sighabs.pdf) separates those polygons from controlled site details. A public download does not itself specify redistribution or third-party live-service reuse rights. The `BIO/WLD_ProvLandScapeViewer_UT83` service says provincial copyright, and the existing NSPRD restricted-map licence expressly covers property records, not this wildlife service. Its 148 public polygons in the wider box therefore remain documented source candidates pending publisher terms for application display. Do not import linked details or exact sensitive sites.
- **Nova Scotia Wetlands and Vegetation Inventory**: [Provincial viewer guidance](https://nsgi.novascotia.ca/plv/help/help.htm) says the circa-2000 interpreted inventory may omit wetlands or include areas that are not wetlands. Live service layers 1 and 2 returned 258 wetland and 355 vegetation polygons in the wider box, but neither the viewer page nor the service supplies a reusable third-party licence. They are not enabled under the property-records licence. A source-specific reuse grant or separately licensed download would resolve this.
- **Provincial special-management-practice zones**: [Public viewer](https://nsgi.novascotia.ca/plv/help/help.htm) explains lynx, marten, coastal flora and moose management layers 5–11. The same wildlife-service reuse gap applies. They are management context, not occurrence points; the broader Cape Breton lynx range should not be presented as a site finding.
- **Predictive Ecosystem Mapping**: [GDD entry 132](https://nsgi.novascotia.ca/gdd/) has a downloadable FileGDB and a model description. It was not converted because its full-province file, categorical dictionary, and display-scale performance have not been validated for this map. A prediction is not a field-verified condition.
- **ECCC critical-habitat points**: Public service layers 1 and 2 expose point geometry including a sensitivity field. The polygon overlays already provide the local screening value without redistributing precise source points. A reviewed sensitivity-aware point policy would be required before adding them.
- **AC CDC, KBA, IBA, breeding-bird atlas, eBird, GBIF and iNaturalist**: Source access entails request, payment, redistribution, record-licence, sensitivity or square-level interpretation issues. None is imported as an authoritative habitat layer. No AC CDC report was ordered.
