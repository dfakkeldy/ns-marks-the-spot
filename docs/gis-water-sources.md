# Water and terrain research layers

The limited sub-tertiary source checks `web/src/data/subTertiaryCoverage.json`
before requesting data. Its 332 envelopes were computed from each polygon part
of all 331 published source features, including WATER parts, on 27 September
2026. The receipt records the exact query. No envelope intersects the Rhodena
rectangle, so the UI reports outside coverage without requesting a blank map.
An envelope intersection does not prove coverage: an empty response within an
envelope stays returned-empty. Regenerate the envelopes if source coverage
changes; these are conservative display guards, not watershed geometry.

Checked 27 September 2026 against the Rhodena study rectangle, 45.69–45.90°N and 61.52–61.28°W. The counts below report records intersecting that rectangle, **not conditions at a turbine, road, parcel or crossing**. Each layer is available on the main map; the source's own coverage and date govern what it can say at Rhodena.

The existing layers already cover WAM predicted wetness/flow, NSTDB water features, the primary–tertiary watershed hierarchy, LiDAR hillshade, forest inventory, Crown treatments and geology. The DFO atlas's wetland class is separate from a classified wetland inventory or field delineation.

## Sources checked but deferred

- **Orthophoto acquisition years and imagery:** [GeoNOVA's June 2026 release](https://geonova.novascotia.ca/new-orthophotography-now-available) confirms 2024 Eastern Nova Scotia imagery in the public [map service](https://nsgiwa.novascotia.ca/arcgis/rest/services/BASE/BASE_NSODB_10k_WM84/MapServer). A spatial query against the study rectangle returns three `PHOTODATE` rows (`2019`, `2021`, `2024`), but **all three have null geometry**. This query cannot establish which, if any, year applies at Rhodena. Orthophoto tiles are [sold under a licence agreement](https://geonova.novascotia.ca/sites/default/files/resource-library/base-mapping_AirPhotos.pdf); the public service alone does not establish unrestricted redistribution or caching rights. Do not label the rectangle “2024.”
- **Freshwater fish records:** [Provincial survey table](https://data.novascotia.ca/d/jgyj-d4fh) supplies dated easting/northing, not latitude/longitude. Its source coordinate reference system was not established for the browser point path; no point was guessed or reprojected.
- **CanLaD disturbance, LiDAR elevation and slope:** [CanLaD](https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/902801fd-4d9d-4df4-9e95-319e429545cc) and the [Nova Scotia Elevation Explorer](https://nsgi.novascotia.ca/datalocator/elevation) provide substantial raster/tile inputs, not a checked lightweight map query or export for these new layers. Elevation and forest disturbance should retain per-tile/annual vintage, datum and model limits when a suitable delivery path exists.
- **Continuous and grab-sample water quality:** The [provincial continuous](https://data.novascotia.ca/d/bkfi-mjgw) and [grab-sample](https://data.novascotia.ca/d/wncu-ppda) tables hold readings keyed to stations, but the [station locations download](https://data.novascotia.ca/d/8ggc-5ang) has no queryable point columns. Readings need an independently verified station join and catchment relationship before local display.
