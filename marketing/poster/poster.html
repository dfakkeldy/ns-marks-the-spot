What other maps don't show you

App & web map, except iPhone · iPadWeb map

Maps from the 1880s

24 of Hugh Fletcher's survey sheets, georeferenced from David Rumsey scans. A sheet can sit a few hundred metres off — and the layer says so.

Pin any scan to the map

Open PDF maps and GeoTIFFs, or georeference a plain scan: tap three matching landmarks and it drapes over today's map. A curved warp suits hand-drawn maps.

NS aerial imagery

The Province's aerial photographs as a background map, under the Province's viewing terms.

Find any parcel

Search by PID or civic address, or tap the map. Inspect the parcel and follow each record back to its source.

Tax-sale notices, mapped

Dated municipal tax-sale notices on live parcel outlines, each with its source named. Public records — not listings or offers.

Measure frontage & area

Distances in metres and kilometres; areas in hectares and acres — for frontage and part-lot checks.

3D terrainWeb map

Tilt and turn the map over province-wide relief, with 1–10× height exaggeration. Not a LiDAR survey.

Provincial layers built in

Property boundaries, Crown lands, flood risk areas, waterfalls, water and roads — under the Province's viewing terms.

Off-grid field kitiPhone · iPad

Save Fletcher tiles for a weak-signal trip. Drop a mark, record a track, and draw lines and polygons.

Every layer shows its source

Each layer names its source and licence. An empty result and a failed source never look the same.

Share & print the view

A shared link reopens the same parcel, layers and map position. Print the map with its sources.

No account. Open source.

No NS Marks account needed. Tracks and imported files are stored on your device. The code is MIT-licensed.
