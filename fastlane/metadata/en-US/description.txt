NS Marks the Spot is a screening and research map for Nova Scotia property. It is built for tax-sale research, land curiosity, and field work — and it is honest about what a map can and cannot prove.

Find a property:
- Search by PID or civic address, or open a shared map link.
- Tap a parcel to open an inspector with the boundary drawn from provincial records.
- Export an evidence note that records what each source answered, and when.

Tax-sale research:
- Browse municipal tax-sale notices as dated records, each tied to the notice it came from.
- Bundled datasets carry the SHA-256 of the file they were built from, so a dataset cannot change without its receipt changing.
- Historical results are kept separate from current notices, so a dated record never reads as a current offering.

Layers and tools:
- Nova Scotia reference layers: property boundaries, Crown lands, aerial imagery, zoning where a municipality publishes it, river and coastal flood zones, well-water risk, contours, waterfalls, and more.
- Measure distance and area.
- Export a framed PDF map with scale and attribution.
- Import your own maps — an old scan can be placed by hand over today's map — and draw your own layers. Both stay on your device.
- Historical overlay support, with saved offline areas that hold overlay sheets. Recently viewed tiles redraw without a connection.
- OpenStreetMap, standard, satellite, and hybrid base maps.

What it will not do:
- Nothing here is a survey, a title search, or legal proof. Every parcel boundary is a drawing of a record, not a line on the ground.
- An empty answer and a failed source are reported differently, and neither is treated as proof that nothing exists.
- Not for route guidance or emergency use.

Every layer names its source, its scale, and its licence. Some provincial services require accepting the Province of Nova Scotia's licence, which you can withdraw in the app at any time. Location access is optional and only shows your position on the map.
