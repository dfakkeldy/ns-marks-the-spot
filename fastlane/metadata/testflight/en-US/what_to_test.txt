This build is about the property-research workflow, not the historical overlay.

Please try:
- Search for a parcel by PID or by civic address, and confirm the map frames it.
- Tap parcels to open the inspector. Check the boundary, the identifiers and the dates shown.
- Export an evidence note from an open parcel and read it. It should say plainly which sources answered, which returned nothing, and which failed.
- Open the tax-sale notices sheet, turn the layer on, and compare notices with the parcels drawn on the map. Switch to historical records and confirm dated results never read as current offerings.
- Toggle reference layers: property boundaries, Crown lands, aerial, flood zones, well-water risk, zoning where offered. Some ask you to accept the provincial licence first — try accepting it, and try withdrawing it from the Map Info sheet.
- Measure a distance and an area.
- Export a framed PDF map and check its scale, its attribution, and the list of layers it says it could not print.
- Import one of your own maps, place it by hand over the modern map, and draw a layer. Both should survive a relaunch, and neither ever leaves your device.
- Compare the OpenStreetMap, standard, satellite and hybrid base maps.
- Start a track recording, put the phone in a pocket or lock the screen for a few minutes, then return. Confirm the track continued (no auto-pause message) and that the system blue location indicator behaved as expected while the recording was off screen.

Known scope for this build:
- The 1885 historical overlay is not visible in this build; its tile hosting is still being wired up. Saved offline areas store historical-overlay tiles, so there is little to save yet.
- NS Aerial and the provincial reference layers are online services. Recently viewed tiles redraw offline until the cache makes room for newer ones.

An empty search result or an empty layer is worth reporting, with the place and the time — but note that the app deliberately reports an empty answer as "nothing returned", never as proof that nothing exists.
