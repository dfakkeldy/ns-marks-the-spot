NS Marks The Spot Point Tupper data have separate data licences; they are not MIT code.
The code's MIT licence does not cover these files or replace any provider's terms.

sites.geojson (project-authored wording):
The project's own descriptions are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). Credit "NS Marks The Spot" and link to this file.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
CC BY 4.0 covers the project's wording only. Facts drawn from third-party documents
(the EverWind EA registration, the Landrie Lake Water Utility EA registration,
Nova Scotia Power, IESO Nova Scotia and news reports) keep their own terms; they are
cited in each record and in docs/point-tupper-sources.md and are not reproduced.

Positions:
- Point Tupper Generating Station, Landrie Lake, Potlotek First Nation and
  Paq'tnkek First Nation: Wikidata (CC0 1.0, public domain),
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The EverWind jetty: the coordinates printed in the EverWind Point Tupper Green
  Hydrogen/Ammonia Project EA registration (December 2022), a public record of the
  Nova Scotia Department of Environment and Climate Change.

The map's other layers (transmission lines, substations, pipelines, wharves, water
features, roads) are the Province's own open datasets under the Open Government
Licence – Nova Scotia, credited on each layer.
