The code's MIT licence does not cover these files or replace any provider's terms.

spots.geojson, rules.json, calendar.json (project-authored wording):
The project's own descriptions, tips, cautions, calendar notes and rule summaries
are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0). Credit "NS Marks The Spot" and link to this file.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
CC BY 4.0 covers the project's wording only. Facts drawn from third-party pages
(tourism listings, park pages, forum and guide reports, regulations, the
Anglers' Handbook and DFO notices) keep their own terms; those pages are cited
in each record's sources and are not reproduced. The rule summaries are an
unofficial paraphrase, not the official text, and have no legal force.

stocked-waters.geojson:
Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Nova Scotia.
https://novascotia.ca/opendata/licence.asp
Derived from NS Fish Hatchery Stocking Records (8e4a-m6fw) and located and
checked with Municipality Boundaries (7bqh-hssn). Coordinates are converted from
the publisher's UTM easting and northing; the zone is inferred by a county check
and recorded per point. Salmon releases are withheld.

No endorsement by any data provider is implied. The data are provided without
warranty; they screen places and seasons and do not establish public access,
title, permission or current rules. Source identities, queries, dates, rejected
records and file hashes are recorded in source.json and stocked-waters.source.json.
