SITE REVIEW - Sentinel-2 build-status checks written into the raw extracts (2026-10-24)
=====================================================================================

pad-check.txt is the machine run (now 2026-07-01/2026-10-24, baseline 2022-06-01/2022-09-30) over
every raw extract plus two approvals kept off the map (Ellershouse 3, Appendix M-P Table 1 p. 7;
Clydesdale Ridge, EA registration Table 6.3 p. 32; both extracted to <scratch> for this check only).
Everything below was also looked at by eye in true-colour crops (the TCI asset, 10 m) around each
position: before/after mosaics, ring radius 40 m. The imagery decides build status only; no position
was taken from it. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2022-2026 (Earth Search, Element 84).

How the results were written into the extracts
-----------------------------------------------
site_check text, from the machine numbers unless noted:
  bare >= 15 of 29 pixels within 30 m  -> "a pad was cleared here by <scene date>"
  otherwise, a cleared disk within 300 m -> "the nearest cleared pad was about <n> m away on <date>"
  otherwise                             -> "no clearing within 300 m by <date>"
build_status: operating (from the proponent, not the imagery), under construction, site work.
The nearest-pad distance is written only where a turbine is expected; at unbuilt Benjamins Mill
positions the nearest bright disk is the substation yard or a road, so they read "no turbine pad".
